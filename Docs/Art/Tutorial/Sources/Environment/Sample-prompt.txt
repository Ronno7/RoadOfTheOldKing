Create a production sprite sheet for The Lost Shrine, a cozy top-down 2D village game. The attached image is ONLY a style reference for the existing terrain: match its extremely simple flat color fills, deliberate chunky pixel shapes and muted pastel-like palette. Do NOT reproduce its tile sheet or labels. Make ONLY FOUR isolated new objects on a genuinely transparent background: a small cream-plaster cottage with a simple muted terracotta roof and timber door/windows; a friendly broad-crowned oak; one squat gray-green boulder; one horizontal rustic two-rail wooden fence with two endposts. Orthographic top-down RPG three-quarter view, front faces visible, no isometric angle. Beautiful clear silhouettes and charming proportions. Strict low-resolution pixel art: pretend the WHOLE sheet is 128x128 native pixels enlarged exactly 8x nearest-neighbor to 1024x1024. Cottage occupies native rectangle x4..67,y4..67 (64x64) in upper left; tree occupies x76..123,y4..67 (48x64) upper right; boulder x12..43,y88..111 (32x24) lower left; fence x64..111,y88..111 (48x24) lower right. Keep ample transparent separation. Every visible pixel block should be 8x8 at output size. Use ONLY these flat opaque hex colors: foliage #60724D #8B9B5E #BECA82 with #384B3C very sparingly; timber #543F36 #826047 #B58C61; roof #794A41 #B86F50 #D99A67; plaster #F1DEB0 #DFC291; stone #77766A #B2AE91. 2-3 tones per material. Roof has broad untextured clay planes and just a FEW short tile seam marks, NOT individual shaded shingles. Oak has 5-7 large joined geometric leafy masses, NOT many tiny leaves. Boulder has two broad planes and one small chip. Timber has flat surfaces, no grain texture. Selective muted edge definition, no thick black outlines. No gradients, no antialias, no painted lighting, no drop shadows or ground shadows, no ground patch, no ambient occlusion, no noise, no dithering, no specular gloss, no flowers or additional props, no text or grid. This is deliberately simple, calm game art to match the reference's visual fidelity.
