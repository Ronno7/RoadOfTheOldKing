<summary><b>Credits and references</b></summary>

<br>

Built by Ronno7. Art and animation made with [PixelLab](https://www.pixellab.ai/); fonts, sound effects and code are original to this project.

**Tools**
- [Unity 6.3](https://unity.com/) with the Universal Render Pipeline (2D)
- [PixelLab](https://www.pixellab.ai/): characters, animation, tiles and UI art
- [fontTools](https://github.com/fonttools/fonttools): compiles the hand-drawn pixel fonts
- [MCP for Unity](https://github.com/CoplayDev/unity-mcp): editor automation during development

**Core inspiration**
- *Dark Souls* (FromSoftware): bonfires, place names, deliberate combat ([Miyazaki on strategic challenge and bonfire relief](https://blog.playstation.com/2011/02/04/dark-souls-qa-variety-is-the-spice-of-death/))
- *The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past* and *A Link Between Worlds* (Nintendo): top-down exploration and dungeon progression
- God of War's Leviathan Axe: throw-and-recall weapon design ([Vince Napoli, Recalling the Leviathan Axe](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/recalling-the-leviathan-axe))
- [*Moonshire*](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2542170/MOONSHIRE/) by Challacade: art direction reference ([Enhancing my game's pixel art!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jBYRUxGmEv0))

**Techniques and tutorials**
- [SLYNYRD Pixelblog](https://www.slynyrd.com/pixelblog-catalogue): top-down tiles, trees, objects, interiors, palettes, walk cycles and attack animation
- [Pixel Frog, Tiny Swords tilemap guide](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords/devlog/1138989/tilemap-guide): elevation, cliff and stair tile relationships
- Radical Fish Games, CrossCode height-map tooling ([1](https://www.radicalfishgames.com/?p=2475), [2](https://www.radicalfishgames.com/?p=3598)): height planning separate from tile art
- [Derek Yu, pixel art tutorial](https://www.derekyu.com/makegames/pixelart.html)
- [Pedro Medeiros, top-down houses](https://www.patreon.com/saint11/posts/top-down-houses-17397188)
- [Time Fantasy colour style guide](https://finalbossblues.com/timefantasy/style-guide/colors/)
- [NVIDIA GPU Gems, waving grass](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-7-rendering-countless-blades-waving-grass): interactive grass
- [Lisa Brown, Hyper Light Drifter level design (GDC 2017)](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2017/Presentations/Brown_Lisa_Applying_3D_Level.pdf)
- [Jolie Menzel, puzzle level design workshop (GDC 2016)](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2016/Presentations/Menzel_Jolie_Level%20Design%20Workshop.pdf) and [Lime Forsberg, Water Temple breakdown](https://www.limeforsberg.com/water-temple)
- [Zhou and Forbes, Data Feel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03800) and [Folmer Kelly, Don't Juice It or Lose It (GDC)](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1021398/Don-t-Juice-It-or): feedback design
- [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 102](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102): readable interactive elements
- Pacing research: [Michael Booth, The AI Systems of Left 4 Dead](https://www.readkong.com/page/the-ai-systems-of-left-4-dead-michael-booth-valve-9664541), [Mark Davies, Examining Game Pace](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/examining-game-pace-how-single-player-levels-tick), [Trinity: intensity ramps](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/trinity-part-6-intensity-ramps), [Koichi Hayashida on Mario level design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-secret-to-i-mario-i-level-design), [The Level Design Book: Undead Burg](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/studies/sp/undead-burg), [PaceMaker (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15001)
- Combat design research: [Anatomy of an Enemy Attack in Dark Souls 3](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/anatomy-of-an-enemy-attack-in-dark-souls-3), [Bart Vossen on melee enemy AI](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/enemy-design-and-enemy-ai-for-melee-combat-systems), [GDKeys, Anatomy of an Attack](https://gdkeys.com/keys-to-combat-design-1-anatomy-of-an-attack/), [The AI of DOOM (2016)](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-), [The Level Design Book](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/combat/enemy), [Mark Foster on Titan Souls](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-one-hit-kills-in-i-titan-souls-i-)
